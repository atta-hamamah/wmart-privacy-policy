Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 9, 2026

Welcome to WMart Mobile ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (wmart.mobile).

1. Information We Collect

To provide a functional and reliable service, we may request access to certain features and collect information including:

  • Location Information: We request access to precise (GPS) and approximate location data (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) to provide delivery tracking, calculate delivery distance, and show you nearby stores.
  • Camera Access: We request camera access (CAMERA) to allow you to scan barcodes and QR codes for quick product searches.
  • Audio Access: We request audio access (RECORD_AUDIO) for voice search features or support channels.
  • Notifications: We request permission to send you push notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) to keep you updated on your order status and alerts.
  • Personal Data: Account registration details such as your name, phone number, email address, and delivery address.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Facilitate user account creation and secure log-in processes.
  • Process, track, and deliver your orders accurately.
  • Optimize app features such as nearby store search and barcode scanning.
  • Send order updates, tracking alerts, and promotional announcements.
  • Improve app performance, fix crashes, and enhance user experience.

3. Sharing and Disclosing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with:

  • Service Providers: Third-party delivery partners, payment processors, and map/analytics API providers (e.g., Google Maps, Firebase) strictly to perform functions on our behalf.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, to comply with legal processes or protect the rights and safety of our users and the public.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information. However, no electronic transmission over the internet or cloud storage can be guaranteed 100% secure.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You can manage your privacy preferences directly in your device's settings:

  • Disable location permissions, camera access, or notifications.
  • Delete or request deletion of your account and personal data from within the app or by contacting support.
